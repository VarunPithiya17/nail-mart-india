The counter

Nail Mart is a counter on Gurudwara Road, Mahavir Enclave, for salons tired of guessing colours from a catalogue. New arrivals are swatched on video and posted on Instagram, so you see the real shade first.

Who we supply

Nail studios and salons, freelance nail artists, nail academies and their students, and resellers across India. Order a single bottle or a full studio setup.

How ordering works

Build a list on this site, send it on WhatsApp, and a real person confirms price and stock. Pay by UPI or bank transfer, and we dispatch. Every order gets a reference you can follow on the Track order page.
